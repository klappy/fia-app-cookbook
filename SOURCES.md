# Sources and access

Authorized reviewers can resolve each claim through the [private claim-to-evidence mapping](https://github.com/klappy/kitchen/blob/main/rail/meals/2026-09-10-fia-app-cookbook/harvest/PASS-001.md). Match the claim ID in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) or the [claim register](claims/CLAIMS.tsv) to its entry there.

The broader [access-controlled evidence index](https://github.com/klappy/kitchen/blob/main/rail/meals/2026-09-10-fia-app-cookbook/BEE-CENSUS.json) tracks source provenance and retrieval state. These private links require authorized repository access; a public reader may see an access error.

Original material remains in its authorized source home. The mapping records witness pointers, observed revisions and review receipts without copying conversations into this public cookbook. Access to the cookbook does not grant access to those sources.

If a pointer cannot be resolved, return an access gap through the authorized coordination path. Do not reconstruct the source or treat it as reviewed. The public text is a sanitized proposal; an evidence review does not itself supply product approval.

Follow the [harvest procedure](harvest/PROCEDURE.md) before adding or revising claims.

## Supporting pass 001

Each public `SUP-001-XX` record in [supporting pass 001](journals/support/SUPPORT-001.md) resolves through the [private support-to-witness map](https://github.com/klappy/kitchen/blob/main/rail/meals/2026-09-10-fia-app-cookbook/development/SUPPORT-001-EVIDENCE.tsv). Match the public record ID to its neutral entry. The map retains the exact reviewed candidate, source revision and witness pointers without copying the semantic inventory or original conversation into the coordination repository.

The [private projection review receipt](https://github.com/klappy/kitchen/blob/main/rail/meals/2026-09-10-fia-app-cookbook/development/SUPPORT-001-REVIEW.md) binds the reviewed public bytes and the all-candidate disposition check. These links require authorized private-repository access. A public access error is not permission to infer a source's contents.

Product entries link to public support records so their rationale is usable without access to private originals. A source-grounded proposal remains different from a validated requirement or current ruling. Relevant withheld meaning and an incomplete item remain explicit private debt; this projection does not claim complete semantic or whole-history coverage.
