# Sources and access

Authorized reviewers can resolve each claim through the [private claim-to-evidence mapping](https://github.com/klappy/kitchen/blob/main/rail/meals/2026-09-10-fia-app-cookbook/harvest/PASS-001.md). Match the claim ID in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) or the [claim register](claims/CLAIMS.tsv) to its entry there.

The broader [access-controlled evidence index](https://github.com/klappy/kitchen/blob/main/rail/meals/2026-09-10-fia-app-cookbook/BEE-CENSUS.json) tracks source provenance and retrieval state. These private links require authorized repository access; a public reader may see an access error.

Original material remains in its authorized source home. The mapping records witness pointers, observed revisions and review receipts without copying conversations into this public cookbook. Access to the cookbook does not grant access to those sources.

If a pointer cannot be resolved, return an access gap through the authorized coordination path. Do not reconstruct the source or treat it as reviewed. The public text is a sanitized proposal; an evidence review does not itself supply product approval.

Follow the [harvest procedure](harvest/PROCEDURE.md) before adding or revising claims.
