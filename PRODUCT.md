# Proposed product direction

This is a source-grounded first proposal for the FIA App, not an approved product specification. Every item remains proposed; current product authority and implementation choices still need confirmation. The [claim register](claims/CLAIMS.tsv) carries the same statements, and [source access](SOURCES.md) explains how authorized reviewers can inspect their evidence.

## Purpose and intended use

The proposed app helps people understand, internalize and express the meaning of Scripture through FIA resources. Translation recording and checking sit outside this proposed initial scope. **FIA-001**

Support communal engagement while allowing a person to use the resources individually. **FIA-002**

## Resource workflow

Make audio, video, images and related resources easy to reach without learning a complicated navigation structure. **FIA-003**

Support a guided flow that can pause for questions or discussion and then resume the relevant resources. **FIA-004**

## Device and delivery constraints

Explore a phone-first, portrait-oriented experience. Broader device support is not established by this proposal. **FIA-005**

Support offline use with deliberate selection, download and removal of resource content to fit device storage. **FIA-006**

Preserve the resource metadata needed by the experience, including timing links, when choosing how content reaches the app. **FIA-009**

Reduce media transfer size for constrained connections while checking that the resulting resources remain usable. **FIA-010**

## Interaction choices still open

A deterministic guided flow is a proposed starting point; AI assistance could be added without making that initial flow depend on it. **FIA-007**

Explore conversational control of navigation and app actions, beyond a chat panel that only answers questions. This is a design possibility, not a confirmed feature or a claim about the first demonstration. **FIA-008**

These proposals do not settle whether AI belongs in the first release, how content delivery will be implemented, or who can approve the product direction. See [current state](STATE.md) for the remaining review boundaries.
