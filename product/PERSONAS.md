# Role hypotheses

These are research models, not validated personas or portraits of actual people. Reviewed historical support describes needs and contexts; role divisions and the proposed exercises below are derived hypotheses. No biography, demographic, quotation, interview, owner or validated metric is invented. One person may occupy several roles.

The [claim register](../claims/CLAIMS.tsv) provides framing. [Supporting pass 001](../journals/support/SUPPORT-001.md) supplies the additional rationale and qualifications; [Sources](../SOURCES.md#supporting-pass-001) resolves exact witnesses for authorized reviewers.

## P-01 — Person guiding a shared session

| Field | Working entry |

| Status | Unvalidated role hypothesis with historical support |

| Context / goal | A group engages with Scripture and related resources; questions and discussion interrupt playback. Shared audible-device use was considered a useful simple case. |
| Needs / tasks | May operate the resource flow, make space for discussion, find or automatically receive a related image, and return to the intended point. Guiding questions should not reveal answers. |

| Research exercise hypothesis | Observe how an actual group distributes device control, repetition, discussion and expression. Test whether a pause/resume flow supports those patterns. |
