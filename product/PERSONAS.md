# Role hypotheses

These are provisional role models for designing research, not validated personas. There are no invented biographies, demographics, quotations or interviews. One person may take more than one role. The [claim register](../claims/CLAIMS.tsv) supplies framing; it does not prove these role divisions.

| ID / role hypothesis | Published seed | Derived context, need and task | Validation gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-01 — Person guiding a shared session | FIA-002, FIA-004 | May operate resources while others engage and discuss; may need to pause and restore shared context. Both role allocation and device control are hypotheses. | Observe how real groups distribute control, questions and turn-taking; evidence not filled. |
| P-02 — Person engaging individually | FIA-001, FIA-002, FIA-003 | May use resources to understand, internalize and express meaning without a group present; proposed task is reaching a relevant resource. | Establish actual goals, navigation needs and circumstances; evidence not filled. |
| P-03 — Person preparing offline content | FIA-005, FIA-006 | May select, download and remove resources before use on a phone. This may be P-01 or P-02 rather than a separate role. | Establish who prepares content, connectivity conditions and storage decisions; evidence not filled. |

Audience knowledge from the designated [oral-theology source home](https://github.com/klappy/oral-theology-kb) is **unassessed in this patch**. Its name does not establish literacy, language, accessibility, cultural or device characteristics of any user. Add a reviewed finding before changing a role model on that basis.
