# Journey drafts

All steps and their ordering below are **derived hypotheses**, not an observed workflow or an approved FIA method sequence. The [published claims](../claims/CLAIMS.tsv) motivate the exercise; [role hypotheses](PERSONAS.md) are also unvalidated. No step is asserted to be implemented.

## J-01 — Engage, discuss, resume

Trigger/context hypothesis: a person guiding a shared session wants to use a resource with others. Seeds: FIA-001 through FIA-004. Related requirement: [R-01](REQUIREMENTS.md#r-01--pause-and-return-to-a-shared-resource).

| Proposed step | Intended outcome | Possible friction to investigate | Recovery hypothesis / evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Find a relevant resource | Reach the intended content | Navigation or resource choice may be unclear | Return to selection without losing context; observe an actual selection task. |
| Engage with the resource | Support understanding and internalization | Group pacing and device control are unknown | Test who controls playback/navigation; do not assume a facilitator role is universal. |
| Pause for a question or discussion | Make room for communal engagement | The relevant position may be lost | Preserve a return point; test this in a prototype. |
| Resume the resource | Continue from the intended point | The group may need repetition or a different resource | Explore replay or reselection; evidence not filled. |

Open: what changes in individual use; what “resume” means for each medium; which actions require assistance. Conversational control remains a possibility under FIA-008, not a prerequisite for this journey. No validated completion metric is available.

## J-02 — Prepare content for offline use

Trigger/context hypothesis: a person anticipates using selected resources without a connection. Seeds: FIA-005, FIA-006, FIA-009, FIA-010. Related requirement: [R-02](REQUIREMENTS.md#r-02--prepare-and-manage-offline-resources).

| Proposed step | Intended outcome | Possible friction to investigate | Recovery hypothesis / evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Select content while connected | Choose what will be available | The complete resource/media boundary is unknown | Evaluate actual Aquifer resource structure before specifying the selection unit. |
| Download and check availability | Prepare a usable offline resource | Interruption, storage and missing linked media are unassessed | Test a retry and an explicit incomplete state; neither exists as a verified feature. |
| Open content offline | Use what was prepared | Content may depend on a remote asset | Run a disconnected exercise against an identified resource and record the result. |
| Remove content when no longer needed | Manage local storage | Deletion scope and accidental removal are unassessed | Investigate confirmation and re-download behavior; do not assume a chosen interaction. |
