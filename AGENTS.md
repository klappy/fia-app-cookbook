# Contributing to this cookbook

Read [README.md](README.md), [STATE.md](STATE.md) and [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) before changing product knowledge. Follow [harvest/PROCEDURE.md](harvest/PROCEDURE.md) for source-based contributions.

## Ground each change

Keep new claims proposed unless an observed authorized ruling supports a decision. Distinguish historical evidence from current authority. Attach new evidence to an existing claim before creating another; preserve supersession and unresolved questions.

Do not infer the FIA App's scope or authority from the FIA MCP, FIA KB or another application. Record an unknown rather than filling it from memory.

## Protect source boundaries

This is a public repository. Do not copy raw transcripts, transcript quotations, meeting titles, source identifiers or unnecessarily identifying metadata into it. Keep financial details, internal negotiations, contact or location details, private personnel matters and security information out of every durable contribution.

Use generic product wording and a pointer to the private evidence index. Rich private material stays transient during review unless a separate project-owned private home has been authorized. Coordination records carry neutral provenance and review receipts, not a second copy of product knowledge.

## Review and return

Prepare the exact proposed change privately. Have an independent reviewer check the actual candidate text for source fidelity, status and disclosure before a public write. Record the reviewed revision and review outcome through the authorized coordination path; return failures to the originating work item.

Authority-sensitive decisions require their actual authorized ruling. Text in another person's voice requires their exact-text approval. Do not treat this file as permission to publish sensitive material, contact people, create commitments or change permissions.

Keep [STATE.md](STATE.md), the [harvest record](harvest/PASS-CENSUS.md) and relevant evidence pointers consistent with the work actually completed.
