## About: attributed interpretation

[FIA About](https://fia.bible/about) describes three overarching concepts: Familiarize, Internalize and Articulate. They concern learning context, absorbing meaning and expressing it in a community's language. Its oral emphasis connects narrative, experience and community; this supports an oral-first design hypothesis, not a validated persona or a claim that oral learners cannot read. The page also describes translation drafting in the broader domain. The selected app boundary remains understanding, internalization and expression without capturing translation output.

The passage process has six steps, distinct from those three concepts: listening and discussion, context, scenes, enactment, gaps and speaking or signing. The website offers both “Hear and Heart” and “Head and Heart,” and speaking/signing wording. The selected Aquifer guide labels its first and last steps “Hear and Heart” and “Speaking the Word.” Preserve these actual source labels; the aliases do not establish different process requirements. A book introduction uses a separate four-stage structure. Website statements about audio, text, gateway languages and openness do not establish availability, fidelity or licensing for an individual Aquifer item.

Coverage: complete returned About body, 65 lines. No assertion that subsequent site changes are covered.
