# Supporting pass 001

These are sanitized historical support records, not approved requirements or current governance. The [ten proposed claims](../../claims/CLAIMS.tsv) remain valid framing. This pass develops their rationale, alternatives, qualifications and open questions; it does not revise their status.

Each record has a public ID that resolves through the [authorized evidence map](../../SOURCES.md#supporting-pass-001). Evidence means reviewed support for what was proposed, observed or transferred historically. It does not establish present feasibility, actual user-research results or a product ruling. Source dates, identities and private context are not reproduced. Some meaning remains withheld or unclear; see [coverage limits](../SUPPORT.md#coverage-and-status).

D-historical records preserve choices described in the evidence and their rationale, with present authority unestablished. C-historical records preserve boundaries of those proposals, not ratified governance. O-closed is a bounded reported observation; O-open remains unresolved. L is a source-grounded lesson rather than measured effectiveness. H records only an actual reported transfer. No E encode or cross-artifact T is claimed. In particular, the AI priority disagreement below is retained as dissent, not promoted to a derived contradiction ruling.
