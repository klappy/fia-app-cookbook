# Supporting pass 003 — scope, delivery and learning from use

Status: sanitized historical proposals, rationales, disagreements and unresolved questions from one reviewed source. These records do not appoint current owners, set dates or budgets, ratify requirements or claim that proposed capabilities have shipped.

Authorized reviewers can resolve each public record through the private projection and witness ledger under the [source-access rules](../../SOURCES.md). Returned discussion threads were interleaved; this presentation does not assert a chronological decision sequence. Private identifying details and quotations are excluded.

The [claim register](../../claims/CLAIMS.tsv) remains proposed. See [current PoC status](../../poc/NARRATION-MEDIA-STATUS.md) for actual implementation and [oral-audience research](../../research/ORAL-AUDIENCE.md) for its separate bounded evidence. Historical testing proposals below are not completed audience research.

Status: historical support / proposal or open question. Attachment: FIA-005.

Account ownership and responsibility for registration were identified as separate coordination tasks that could run before the app was finished. Distinguish store-account ownership, developer access and task responsibility; no current person/account is appointed here.

## SUP-003-15
Status: historical support / proposal or open question. Attachment: supporting development question; no new requirement ratified.

## SUP-003-26
Status: historical support / proposal or open question. Attachment: supporting development question; no new requirement ratified.
