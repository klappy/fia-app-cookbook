# Supporting knowledge

DOLCHEOT supports evidence, reasoning and continuity. It does not replace the claim register, roadmap or actual work queue. Initial records below contain an observation about published framing and open research threads; they do not claim new source findings.

| Type | Use only when supported |
|---|---|
| D — Decision | An actual authorized decision and its rationale are observed. |
| O — Observation | A fact is directly observed; state exactly what the observation establishes. |
| L — Learning | Evidence supports a lesson, including what changed in understanding or practice. |
| C — Constraint | A governing boundary is known and can be cited. |
| H — Handoff | A real transfer occurred; retain its actual receipt and scope. |
| E — Encode | An actual encode receipt exists; do not invent completion or quality scores. |
| O-open — Open thread | An unresolved question or follow-up is explicitly retained. |
| T — Tension | A derived contradiction between artifacts has revision references and advisory status; it is not an invented ruling. |
